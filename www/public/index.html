ordinal-notation

Constructive Agda development of ordinal notations, extracted from echo-types: Brouwer ordinals, the Veblen hierarchy up to a defined Γ₀, and a Buchholz-style term order with machine-checked well-foundedness under --safe --without-K. Frozen hand-off record; order-type fidelity to ψ₀(Ω_ω) is open.

This site is served from the www/public/ category of the repository's site-operations bundle. Operational material (DNS, TLS, headers, profiles, tests, runbooks) is deliberately not part of the published surface.
